- **Detection rules** (heuristic). A card matching both sides counts once, as a payoff unless enablers are below `minEnablers` and payoffs are at their minimum. Primary/secondary = the two strongest with assembly >= 0.6; `all` = every family meeting its minimums. `singleCardDependence` = the defId of the only payoff, else the only enabler.

## After Task 9 (lines)

Measured 2026-11-13 on the fixture pool and fixture meta (300 lists). `tests/engine/lines.test.ts` 15 tests.

- **API.** `generateLines(deck, ctx) -> LineReport` (`src/engine/lines/lines.ts`), plus `FOLD_RISK_SHARE = 0.15` (heuristic). Uses plans, maths, features, network and meta only (no scoring/rating/novelty). 40-75 ms per reference deck (most of it the energy model when uncached). An empty deck (or only unknown cards) gives all sections empty.
- **Sections.** `turns`: always 6 lines (turns 1-6) for a non-empty deck, `probability` = P(a play) from `energyModel` (provenance `derived`); text names the likely plays grouped by cost, the primary plan's pieces costing that turn, and the key turn's assembly. `assembly[0]` is the primary plan headline (`probability` = `PlanMatch.assembly`), then one line per piece (up to 8) with the odds without it (`matchPlan` on the deck minus the piece) and the same-side substitutes in `cardIds[1..]`; then the secondary plan the same way. `sequencing`: ordered pairs, `cardIds[0]` is played first (rules: On Reveal repeaters by trigger, last-card copiers, next-card effects, cost reducers, On Reveals acting on own cards, play triggers, full/alone conditions; rules 4-6 need a non-heuristic synergy reason on the edge), at most 3 per acting card and 10 in all. `locations`: the plan's `takesLocations`, tall (strong `sameLocation` edges), wide (summon/gen-board traits), move axis, lockdown role, play restrictions. `packages`: every `PlanReport.all` family plus strong-edge components. `oneAway`: plans one enabler or payoff short; candidate cards (released, not in deck, owned when `ctx.owned`) are in the text only, `cardIds` stay in the deck. `field`: top 5 archetypes by `share` (games), plan = `planId` or primary plan detected on `sample` (cached per db). `tech`: up to 3 tech roles uncovered by the deck, ranked by summed archetype share folding to them, 1-2 cards each with what they replace (least-connected off-plan, non-tech, unlocked card). `risks`: no plan at 60%, single-card dependence (also for the closest plan when none qualifies), folds with `fieldShare >= 0.15`, up to 5 net-negative pairs.
- **Card ids.** All lines except `field` and `tech` name only deck cards; `field`/`tech` also name outside cards (the opponent's tech, proposed tech).
- **Numbers.** Six reference decks share 0.0% of line texts pairwise (threshold 15%). Fixture meta: On Reveal denial is the largest uncovered role (42.6% of games fold to it), Add lock 36.9%.
- **Known gaps.** Turn lines group likely plays by cost but cannot say how many of each cost (the energy model returns interchangeable cards, not the combo). Sequencing is heuristic and over-includes broad text ties (Magneto deck: "Anti-Polar Magneto before Jane Foster Fractured Frontier" via a broad buff). Location shape is a summary, not a simulation. Field "what to hold" advice is generic (hold payoffs until their tech is played or split lanes). No win-rate matrix. Tech replacement picks the same weakest card for every role.
