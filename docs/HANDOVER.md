- **Features hook.** `fixtureHooks.compute` in `tests/helpers/fixture-db.ts` is null, so `features()` is undefined and `edges()` empty. Task 5 sets it and calls `resetFixtureDb()`. `src/ingest/features.ts` is a stub.

## After Task 4 (card-text parser)

Measured 2026-11-13 on the fixture pool (511 released, 504 with text).

- **API.** `parseCard(card) -> { clauses, unparsed }` (`src/engine/text/parse.ts`, also re-exports `TRIGGERS`, `EFFECT_KINDS`, `TARGETS`); `classifyPhrase(text)` maps a noun phrase to a target. `loadOverrides(json)` + `applyOverrides(card, parsed, overrides) -> { clauses, unparsed, staleOverride }` (`overrides.ts`; the JSON is `{ about, cards: { defId: { reviewedText, note, clauses } } }`). `coverage(cards, parsedById)` and `attributeMismatches(card, clauses)` (`coverage.ts`). Task 5 should feed features from `applyOverrides(card, parseCard(card), loadOverrides(json))`.
- **Clause shape.** One clause per sentence (two for "On Reveal and When Destroyed"); clauses can have zero effects (set-up sentences). Text without a header has trigger `static`, or a sentence trigger from `PASSIVE` (`on_own_play`, `static_cost`, `play_restriction`, `moveable`, ...). Objective: trigger `objective`, the requirement is a condition `obj:*`, the reward is the effects. `effect.selectors` holds `sel:*` tags of that effect's phrase, including `sel:other` ("your other cards"). Magnitudes are unsigned for `afflict` and `cost_reduce`.
- **Target conventions** are written at the top of `tests/fixtures/clause-audit.json` and in `vocab.ts`: `self`, `own_here`/`enemy_here`/`all_here`, `*_anywhere` (named locations count as anywhere), `own_other_locations`, `own_hand`/`own_deck`/`enemy_hand`/`enemy_deck`, `both` (both players, hands or decks), `own`/`enemy` (a side, no zone), `*_player`, trigger subjects (`own_next_played`, `own_played_card`, `enemy_played_card`, `own_moved_card`, `enemy_moved_card`, `own_drawn_card`), `location`. Negative Power on any card is `afflict`; `copy_card` targets where the copy lands; `resurrect` whose card returns.
- **Numbers.** Clause audit: 123 hand-read cards, parser alone 123/123 trigger+kind+target. The audit was also the development set, so this overstates held-out accuracy: a review of the other 381 cards' output found about 25 target errors, fixed in the parser, and the ones still known are listed below (about 2% of cards). Fully read sentences: 494/504 cards by the parser alone (prototype 93.5%), 504/504 with the 16 overrides. Attribute cross-check: 8 disagreements (Cannonball, Khonshu, Ozymandias, Spider-Ham tokens; Kingpin, Techno-Organic Virus, Web Sling magnitudes).
- **Known gaps.** Namora reads `own_anywhere` (the condition "alone at another location" hides the location); Storm Horseman buffs only `own_hand` (misses "here"); Web Sling's "+1 Power when it moves" is folded into `grant_moveable`; Prodigy copies `all_anywhere` (should be the card in front, `own_here`); Sersi gets a spurious `cost_increase`; Nightmare and Spider-Ham get two `copy_text` effects; Terrax's "losing player's deck" is read `enemy_deck`; Debrii and Shanna "add to each location" are read as your side (text does not say, mechanics §6 q6). Of the 46 cards of mechanics §5 only 16 have overrides (those the parser cannot read plus Doctor Octopus FF, found wrong in review); the rest rely on the parser.

Ingest: `syncFeatures(db, log, { now, root })` returns a summary, `readOverrides(root)`, `strongest(edges, n)`.
- **Fixture hook is on.** `fixtureDb()` computes features and edges at test time with `fixtureOverrides()` (exported from `tests/helpers/fixture-db.ts`). Set `fixtureHooks.compute = null` and call `resetFixtureDb()` for a bare database.
- **Axes.** `CardFeatures.axes[id] = { e, p, heuristic }`, `e`/`p` are 1 (text reading), 0.5 (cost or Power guess) or 0. 32 axes; `heuristic` on the axis means one side can be a guess. Deck-level checks that are not pairs (payoff with no enabler in the deck, play restriction unreachable) are not computed here: Tasks 6-7 use `axes` and `restriction` for them.
- **Tech roles** (`TechRole` ids): ongoing-removal, text-removal, on-reveal-denial, power-reset, cost-lock, add-lock, reduction-immunity, big-card-removal, sweeper, destroy-lock, hand-attack, deck-attack, created-tax, displacement, lockdown, protection. `deck-attack` and `created-tax` go beyond the spec §8.2 list (theory §3 rows).
- **Edges.** One per unordered pair, `a < b` by defId; direction lives in the reasons (`label` says who enables whom; `detail` quotes both texts as `Name: "sentence"`, or `Name (cost/power, no ability)`). Reason `value` is the fan-out weighted strength `strength / log2(2 + max(fanA, fanB))` (prototype used the geometric mean; changed after the audit, see below). Pair synergy = (best family + 0.5 x other families) / 1.2, capped at 1; anti the same; `score = synergy - anti`. A family counts once per pair (`one_cost` with `filter:cost`, `big_power` with `filter:power`, `discard` with `discard:selector_hit`, `destroy` with `mass:destroy_feeds`). Heuristic reasons (axes bounce, cost_reduction, energy_ramp, sequencing, multi_play, location_control, go_wide, alone, or any side read from cost or Power) cap synergy at 0.49. `sameLocation` follows the best synergy reason (else any anti) being location-scoped.
- **Edge audit.** `tests/fixtures/edge-audit.json`, seed 20261118, 60 strong edges hand-judged: **53 true, 4 lenient, 3 false: strict 88.3%, lenient 95.0%** (prototype 70% strict). Four earlier rounds (seeds 20261113-20261117: 50%, 72%, 77%, 92%, 88%) were used to fix rules and are not counted. The three false patterns (Luke Cage as a self-affliction payoff, printed negative Power counted as affliction, board effects picking skills) were fixed after this sample: strong set -18 / +4, the 4 new ones read and true; that final set has not been re-sampled. Tests: sampled true/lenient edges stay strong, false ones are not; 15 known-true strong; 15 unrelated not strong; 5 anti examples.
- **Quality** is heuristic: `50 + 4 x (Power - pool mean for cost - 2 if drawback) + 4 x text power-equivalents (capped -6..10, halved when conditional, doubled when scaled) + measured`, measured = `winDelta x 100 x games / (games + 50 x lists)`, clamped to +/-10. Pool means reproduce theory §1.5 (2.15 ... 8.59, 500 characters). Not calibrated against meta win rates.
- **Known gaps.** Strong edges favour precision over recall: broad true relations stay below 0.5 (Zabu x a 4-Cost card 0.39-0.495, Wong x an On Reveal card about 0.21, Mockingbird x a card creator 0.405). Remaining lenient patterns: conditional enablers (Namorita "if this is your only card here"), deck buffs x deck summoners (Agent Venom x Malekith), Phoenix Force merging into a revived card. Parser gaps of Task 4 flow through (Terrax reads `enemy_deck`, Debrii/Shanna as own side). `anti:filler_vs_space` from the prototype was dropped (weakest rule in its hand-check). `MetaArchetype.planId` is still null: tagging archetypes with plan families needs Task 6's `sync-data/plans.json`. The coverage report is printed and stored in `sync_state('features').info`; no UI yet.

## After Task 6 (maths and plans)

Measured 2026-11-13 on the fixture pool. Suite 311 tests (`maths` 13, `plans` 10, one new features-ingest test).

- **Maths API** (`src/engine/maths/`). `mulberry32(seed)`, `shuffleInPlace(xs, rnd)` (`rng.ts`). `cardsSeen`, `pAtLeastOne`, `pAll`, `pOneOfEach`, `choose`, `DECK_SIZE`, `OPENING_HAND` (`hypergeo.ts`): all clamp input, return [0, 1], never NaN; `deckSize` defaults to 12. Exact theory §1.2 values reproduced to 4 decimals.
- **Energy model** (`energy.ts`): `energyModel(deck, db, seed = 20261113, games = 4000)`, `energySignature(deck, db)`, `TOTAL_ENERGY = 21`, `TURNS = 6`. Arrays by turn have length 7 with index 0 unused; `curve` has length 8 (0..7+). Greedy as in `draw_odds.py` (max energy, then fewest cards, then most expensive), plus HEURISTICS: own `model.selfDiscount` lowers the card's cost, `model.extraEnergy` is permanent for `energy_max_player` cards else next turn only, 0-cost cards always played. `likelyPlays[t]` lists every deck card whose signature is in the most frequent play (equal signatures are interchangeable). Cached by signature + seed + games (500 entries). Measured at seed 7, 4,000 games: 20.24 / 17.20 / 13.51 vs research 20.28 / 17.21 / 13.50; about 60 ms per uncached deck. Unknown cards are skipped; an empty deck gives 0 spent and `pNoPlay` 1.
- **Plans** (`src/engine/plans/detect.ts`): `detectPlans(deck, ctx)`, `matchPlan(plan, deck, db)`, `drawAdjust(deck, db, keyTurn) -> { extra, deckSize }`, `foldInfo(db, role) -> { cards (top 6 by list share), fieldShare }` (cached per db), `checkPlans(plans, roles)`, `atomsOf`, `compileAtom`, `matchesAny`, `nameList`, `PLAN_THRESHOLD = 0.6`.
- **plans.json pattern grammar.** Patterns are `&`-joined atoms: a `traitsOf` id, `axis:<id>.e|p` (text side, strength 1 only), `role:<techRole>`, `cost:lo-hi`, `power:lo-hi` (skills never match these), `creates:no_ability` (a `Card.tokens` entry with empty text, e.g. Rhino's Rock). A test checks every trait atom is emitted by some released card and every axis exists. This goes beyond "trait ids only": flat traits cannot express payoffs such as Patriot (`buff_own ongoing`), so axes carry most payoffs.
- **Detection rules** (heuristic). A card matching both sides counts once, as a payoff unless enablers are below `minEnablers` and payoffs are at their minimum. Assembly = `pOneOfEach(enablers, payoffs, keyTurn, extra, deckSize)`, or `pAtLeastOne` of the one side when the other side's minimum is 0 (only `cost-diversity`). `extra` = half the `model.extraSeen` of deck cards cheaper than the key turn, rounded down, at most 3; `deckSize = max(12, cards)`, shuffled-in cards not counted. `strength = assembly x payoffWeight`. Primary/secondary = the two strongest with assembly >= 0.6; `all` = every family meeting its minimums. `singleCardDependence` = the defId of the only payoff, else the only enabler.
- **Results.** Reference decks: zombie horde (0.95 assembly), fantomex destroy (secondary discard), magneto and arthur put-into-play, patriot no-ability, kluh cost-diversity (strength 0.45; disruption 5 x Kluh is 0.58, below the threshold). `pnpm sync features` now tags archetypes: 30 of 48 get a `planId` on the real database (`tagArchetypes(db, cardDb)`, summary field `archetypes`). Untagged: Created Cards, Vampire Killmonger, Galacti, Virus, Surfer, Merlin, Thanos, Lancelot, the Cerebro lists, Zoo, Venus, On Reveal.
- **Known gaps.** No family for created cards, skills or "spend all energy" (Vampire Killmonger, Merlin), which are big meta archetypes; theory §2 has none either. Disruption's payoff is only `axis:big_power.e`. `put-into-play` payoffs are any 3-4 Cost card with 5+ Power, `extra-turn` enablers are Magik's shape (`loc_change_location&cond:turn_number`). Assembly ignores the per-plan "at least N" minimums beyond one of each. Energy model ignores board space and generated cards. `payoffWeight` values (0.6-0.9) are not calibrated.

## After Task 9 (lines)

Measured 2026-11-13 on the fixture pool and fixture meta (300 lists). `tests/engine/lines.test.ts` 15 tests.

- **API.** `generateLines(deck, ctx) -> LineReport` (`src/engine/lines/lines.ts`), plus `FOLD_RISK_SHARE = 0.15` (heuristic). Uses plans, maths, features, network and meta only (no scoring/rating/novelty). 40-75 ms per reference deck (most of it the energy model when uncached). An empty deck (or only unknown cards) gives all sections empty.
- **Sections.** `turns`: always 6 lines (turns 1-6) for a non-empty deck, `probability` = P(a play) from `energyModel` (provenance `derived`); text names the likely plays grouped by cost, the primary plan's pieces costing that turn, and the key turn's assembly. `assembly[0]` is the primary plan headline (`probability` = `PlanMatch.assembly`), then one line per piece (up to 8) with the odds without it (`matchPlan` on the deck minus the piece) and the same-side substitutes in `cardIds[1..]`; then the secondary plan the same way. `sequencing`: ordered pairs, `cardIds[0]` is played first (rules: On Reveal repeaters by trigger, last-card copiers, next-card effects, cost reducers, On Reveals acting on own cards, play triggers, full/alone conditions; rules 4-6 need a non-heuristic synergy reason on the edge), at most 3 per acting card and 10 in all. `locations`: the plan's `takesLocations`, tall (strong `sameLocation` edges), wide (summon/gen-board traits), move axis, lockdown role, play restrictions. `packages`: every `PlanReport.all` family plus strong-edge components. `oneAway`: plans one enabler or payoff short; candidate cards (released, not in deck, owned when `ctx.owned`) are in the text only, `cardIds` stay in the deck. `field`: top 5 archetypes by `share` (games), plan = `planId` or primary plan detected on `sample` (cached per db). `tech`: up to 3 tech roles uncovered by the deck, ranked by summed archetype share folding to them, 1-2 cards each with what they replace (least-connected off-plan, non-tech, unlocked card). `risks`: no plan at 60%, single-card dependence (also for the closest plan when none qualifies), folds with `fieldShare >= 0.15`, up to 5 net-negative pairs.
- **Card ids.** All lines except `field` and `tech` name only deck cards; `field`/`tech` also name outside cards (the opponent's tech, proposed tech).
- **Numbers.** Six reference decks share 0.0% of line texts pairwise (threshold 15%). Fixture meta: On Reveal denial is the largest uncovered role (42.6% of games fold to it), Add lock 36.9%.
- **Known gaps.** Turn lines group likely plays by cost but cannot say how many of each cost (the energy model returns interchangeable cards, not the combo). Sequencing is heuristic and over-includes broad text ties (Magneto deck: "Anti-Polar Magneto before Jane Foster Fractured Frontier" via a broad buff). Location shape is a summary, not a simulation. Field "what to hold" advice is generic (hold payoffs until their tech is played or split lanes). No win-rate matrix. Tech replacement picks the same weakest card for every role.

## After Task 7 (scoring, rating, novelty)

Measured 2026-11-13 on the fixture pool. Suite 355 tests (`novelty` 12, `rating` 6, `scoring` 11).

- **Calibration target of spec §12 NOT reached.** With `src/engine/rating/constants.ts` as committed: 88 of the 200 most-played fixture lists rate 60+ (44%, target 95%); 41 of 50 random piles (seed 20261113) rate under 40 (82%, target 100%); mean gap about 20 points. - **Rating** (`rating/rate.ts`, `rating/deck.ts`, `rating/constants.ts`). `rateDeck`, `planLinkage(db, plan)` (share of enabler x payoff pairs with score >= 0.2; plan strength is discounted by it), `primaryOf`. - **Scoring** (`scoring/scorer.ts`). In-deck cards are scored leave-one-out; the IN_DECK gate fires only for a second copy. Tuned meta part: lists sharing at least o1 - 2 cards (top 30), inclusion shrunk with 5 pseudo-lists toward the overall share. `scoreAll` over 511 released cards runs well inside 400 ms after warm-up.
